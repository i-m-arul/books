# 21.05.91

### Fracture Novels — Book II

By **Arul Anand**

---

> Everyone remembers what happened. Few remember how ordinary it felt before it did.

On the night of May 21, 1991, history fractured in thirteen seconds.

*21.05.91* is a gripping historical thriller that revisits the assassination of Rajiv Gandhi not as a single act of violence, but as the inevitable outcome of overlooked warnings, political fractures, and silences that stretched across years.

Set against the turbulent backdrop of post-1984 India, the novel traces the aftermath of Indira Gandhi's assassination, the rise of Rajiv Gandhi, and the escalating tensions linking New Delhi, Tamil Nadu, and Sri Lanka. Through journalists, intelligence officers, political aides, and bystanders, the story reveals how danger was sensed, whispered about, and quietly dismissed.

This is not a tale of ignorance but of awareness without action.

Blending meticulously researched history with powerful fictionalized perspectives, *21.05.91* explores how history often moves through unnoticed spaces, carried forward by ordinary moments and missed decisions. It is a meditation on inevitability, perception, and the small fractures that shape destiny.

A compelling sequel in The Fracture Novels series, this book challenges readers to ask: *What if history doesn't need permission — only space?*
