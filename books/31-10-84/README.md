# 31.10.84: A Nation on the Edge

### Fracture Novels — Book I

By **Arul Anand**

---

> A nation appears united — until a single moment exposes its deepest fractures.

*31.10.84: A Nation on the Edge* is a political fiction novel inspired by the assassination of India's Prime Minister and the chain of events that followed. As power shifts overnight, fault lines emerge across politics, bureaucracy, intelligence agencies, and the streets of a shaken country.

Told through multiple perspectives, the story explores how decisions made in silence, warnings ignored, and loyalties questioned culminate in an act that alters history. What unfolds is not just the story of an assassination, but of a system under strain — where ideology, fear, and ambition collide.

Blending real historical context with fictionalized insight, this novel asks a haunting question: *Was the tragedy inevitable — or the result of fractures no one chose to see?*
